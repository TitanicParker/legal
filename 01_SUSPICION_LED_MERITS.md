# 01 — Suspicion-Led Merits

## Governing status

This is the controlling merits route for `TitanicParker/legal`.

The repository is governed by the **suspicion**. This file does not neutralise it. It states the strongest version that the evidence presently supports, identifies the graver limb still under forensic test, and gives the evidence room to accumulate before rebuttal is applied.

> **Take the strongest version of the suspicion seriously, test it against the whole record, strengthen it where the evidence strengthens it, narrow it where the evidence narrows it, and abandon any limb the evidence defeats.**

---

# 1. Strongest presently established centre

An integrated painful-feet–gait–tone/movement problem reached Neurology in 2017. Neurology observed a striking footdrop-like lower-limb phenomenon it considered suggestive of dystonic posture rather than true foot drop, and the same dystonic-looking gait was used affirmatively in the Parkinsonian diagnostic project. The final specialist synthesis nevertheless placed the painful-foot/leg phenomena into an alternative branch in which they might be completely separate or secondary peripheral neuropathy and left the relationship `Not clear`.

Thereafter the same clinical system repeatedly generated evidence crossing that separation: treatment withdrawal changed tension, feet and gait; a contemporaneous October 2017 account recorded a `twisting urge in my left foot`; the treating neurologist later recorded rapid focal outside-left-foot change during withdrawal; a later footdrop-like phenomenon was classified as Parkinsonian after directed distal testing; GP records again related painful/clawing feet to tone, gait and Parkinson's; Neurology later reasoned about OFF-related leg pain/shuffling; and Sinemet was expressly recognised to improve the feet from a pain perspective, with further benefit expected but constrained by dyskinesia.

The presently available record demonstrates repeated **local updating** of particular phenomena. It does not presently demonstrate a corresponding **global reconstruction** of the original relationship among painful feet, dystonic/Parkinsonian lower-limb behaviour, structural mechanics, loading and neurological treatment state.

> **If independent expert evidence establishes the relevant neurological duty, the centre of the alleged wrongdoing is the specialist partition of an integrated painful-foot/neurological lower-limb problem into separately managed explanatory streams without adequate demonstrated integration, followed by continuing stewardship in which accumulating evidence repeatedly crossed that partition without a presently demonstrated whole-case reconstruction of the founding relationship.**

The mechanism is:

> **inadequate integration → documentary disposition/closure → loss or diffusion of relational ownership → repeated crossing evidence → local updating without demonstrated global reconstruction → continuing stewardship of the resulting information state → later reliance on accumulated coherence.**

# 2. The graver suspicion remains active

The patient gives a specific retrospective account that immediately before discharge he challenged Neurology for not dealing with the feet for which he had entered care. The surviving specialist record does not contain that exchange.

The unresolved forensic question is what happened to the document around that moment.

The suspicion is that consequential language and explanatory routing — including `leg pain`, `burning`, possible secondary peripheral neuropathy, possible alcohol relation and `Not clear` — may have been added or materially reformulated after the Protest, while the dystonic lower-limb phenomenon was used prospectively in the Parkinsonian diagnostic project.

The decisive question is **authorship time**:

> **Did those consequential elements already exist before the Protest, or were any first added or materially reformulated afterwards?**

If native version/audit evidence supports the latter, the case becomes more serious: not merely inadequate integration, but possible knowing documentary construction of closure after notice that the presenting problem remained unresolved.

That limb is not presently established. It remains active because the repository is governed by the suspicion, not by the narrowest allegation already capable of proof.

# 3. Evidential propositions carrying the suspicion

1. **Integrated referral** — the GP referred an integrated feet–gait–tone/movement problem.
2. **Neurological recognition** — Neurology itself characterised the lower-limb phenomenon as potentially dystonic rather than true foot drop.
3. **Diagnostic use** — the dystonic-looking gait was used affirmatively in the Parkinsonian/DaTscan project.
4. **Affirmative partition** — the final synthesis placed painful-foot/leg phenomena into a potentially separate/peripheral-neuropathy branch and left the relationship `Not clear`.
5. **Visible relational deficit** — the surviving July record does not presently demonstrate the complete relationship-directed work by which that partition was earned.
6. **Early treatment-state crossing** — procyclidine withdrawal altered tension, feet and gait; the treating neurologist later recorded rapid focal foot change during withdrawal.
7. **Independent reintegration** — GP records later again connected painful/clawing feet with tone, gait and Parkinson's.
8. **Neurology crosses its own boundary** — later Neurology classified a footdrop-like phenomenon as Parkinsonian and reasoned about OFF-related pain/shuffling.
9. **Pain enters dopaminergic reasoning** — the treating neurologist later recognised Sinemet-related foot-pain benefit and expected further dopaminergic benefit.
10. **Same-clinician stewardship** — the originating neurologist remained substantially involved across the period and later relied on the accumulated longitudinal history to explain the care.

Read cumulatively:

> **An integrated neurological/foot problem was partitioned; the same clinical system subsequently generated repeated evidence reconnecting neurological state and the feet; individual episodes were recognised and treated, but the presently available record does not demonstrate a corresponding explicit global reconstruction of the relationship that had originally been partitioned.**

# 4. What does not answer the centre

The repo should not allow evidence to rebut a case it no longer makes.

- Real structural disease rebuts `all pain was neurological`; it does not answer whether mixed structural/neurological truth was adequately integrated.
- Successful Parkinsonian diagnosis and treatment rebut `the whole admission was a failure`; they do not answer whether one central presenting relationship was inadequately integrated.
- Dyskinesia/toxicity rebut `simply give more dopamine`; they do not answer whether treatment-state evidence required reformulation.
- Pregabalin benefit supports mixed mechanisms; mixed mechanisms do not remove the need for relational ownership.
- Later genetics supports the Parkinsonian/Parkin diagnosis; it does not resolve the historical painful-foot relationship.
- Later reassessment rebuts `nothing was reconsidered`; it does not by itself establish global reconstruction.
- PAR/SIMT is real adverse institutional evidence; its weight depends on what it actually reconstructed and independently tested.

Full defence analysis belongs in `80_DEFENCE_AND_LIMITATIONS.md`, not inside every merits paragraph.

# 8. What can defeat the centre

The real defeat conditions are concentrated:

1. independent expert evidence that the 2017 relational assessment was clinically adequate;
2. proof of a later genuine whole-case reconstruction that the repository has missed;
3. proof of durable relational ownership across the relevant services;
4. objective evidence materially vindicating the founding neuropathy/separation pathway in a way the present record does not capture;
5. an appropriately independent later review showing that the same integration/stewardship allegation was actually reconstructed and answered.

The graver construction limb has its own decisive test: native authorship/version/audit evidence.

# 9. Controlling proposition

> **The limb was neurologically meaningful when it supplied diagnostic evidence. The painful-foot relationship was then given a potentially separate disposition. The same treating system later generated repeated evidence crossing that separation without a presently demonstrated whole-case reconstruction. The governing suspicion asks whether that was merely an imperfect but reasonable mixed-mechanism pathway, or whether specialist authority first created and then preserved an explanatory architecture that displaced an unresolved neurological problem and later helped validate itself.**
