# 12 — Whole-Case Evidential Genealogy

## Status and purpose

**Active whole-case orientation file.**

This repository cannot responsibly discuss the case by starting from the finished July 2017 specialist account as though that document were the beginning of the evidence.

The central rule is:

> **The final specialist record is not the start of the case. It is itself an output of earlier evidence, encounters, decisions and document construction.**

The whole case must therefore be read genealogically: what entered the world first, what was later observed, what was interpreted, what was written, what became portable, what was inherited, what was independently re-tested, what was later defended, and what is now being challenged through provenance and data-governance routes.

This file exists to make that genealogy visible.

It does not replace `REPAIRED_CANONICAL_V2.md`, the patient statement, the focused July/provenance audits, the same-clinician ledger, or expert review. It tells a reader how those objects fit together.

---

# 1. The true evidential start

The evidential start is not Parkinson's disease, PRKN, neuropathy, Podiatry or the final discharge synthesis.

The earliest patient-specific object is the lived and GP-observed problem that preceded specialist formulation:

> **painful abnormal feet + abnormal gait + increasing tone/movement difficulty + deteriorating function.**

The GP progressively brought those features together before Neurology. That pre-specialist material is therefore an independent comparator against which the later specialist representation can be tested.

The patient's experiential starting point is even simpler:

> **My feet hurt and walking was becoming intolerable.**

The distinction matters because later terminology must not be projected backwards merely because it eventually became part of the record.

---

# 2. What may have been made to look like the start

A later reader receiving a polished specialist discharge product can easily experience it as the authoritative beginning of the story.

But the July 2017 final product is a composite documentary object. It follows:

1. the longstanding bodily problem;
2. the GP's integrated referral and pre-specialist observations;
3. the inpatient encounter and bedside findings;
4. the patient's own communications and experience of the admission;
5. the alleged pre-discharge Protest;
6. physical discharge;
7. later DaTscan/addendum activity;
8. subsequent transcription/finalisation/confirmation processes.

The finished document may therefore function as a **documentary start** for downstream readers while not being the **evidential start** of the case.

That distinction is fundamental to the construction hypothesis.

# 3. Evidence genealogy classes

Every material later item should be classified by genealogy, not merely by date.

## A. Primary patient-state evidence

What the patient experienced, noticed, said, did, tolerated, improved with, worsened with, or remembers from the encounter.

This includes contemporaneous patient communications and retrospective patient evidence about earlier events. Retrospective evidence is still evidence; it is not automatically equivalent to contemporaneous documentation.

## B. Independent clinical observation

A clinician's examination, investigation result, treatment-state observation or other patient-specific finding whose existence does not depend on accepting an earlier explanatory theory.

Examples include abnormal gait, dystonic-looking lower-limb behaviour, structural foot lesions, medication withdrawal effects, normal distal power testing, OFF-related observations and treatment response.

## C. Interpretive clinical proposition

A clinician-generated explanation placed upon observations: Parkinsonian, dystonic, structural, neuropathic, OFF-related, alcohol-related, mixed or otherwise.

Interpretation is evidence of what was thought; it is not automatically proof that the explanation was correct.

## D. Documentary output

A summary, referral, discharge synthesis, clinic letter, complaint response or other representation that selects and organises earlier material into a portable information state.

The output must be analysed separately from the observations from which it claims to arise.

## E. Descendant narrative statement

A later statement whose wording, assumption or explanatory structure may substantially descend from an earlier record rather than from fresh independent assessment.

Repetition can show continuity without proving independent confirmation.

## F. Independent re-evaluation

A later clinician or reviewer who actually re-examines the patient, underlying source material or founding reasoning and generates an assessment not merely inherited from the existing summary.

Independence must be demonstrated, not assumed from seniority, title or institutional separation.

## G. Governance/review output

Complaint findings, PAR/SIMT conclusions, QPS responses, regulatory decisions and similar institutional representations.

These are evidence of what the reviewing body concluded and how it processed the case. Their weight on the underlying clinical issue depends on what they actually examined.

## H. Data-rights/provenance intervention

Article 15 access/provenance requests, Article 16 completion/rectification requests, Article 18 restriction activity, DPC complaints and related processing questions.

These are not merely administrative side-events. Where they seek authorship history, source genealogy, accuracy markers, supplementary statements or the evidence base of later review, they directly test the information architecture under examination.

# 4. The whole-case sequence

The repository should teach the case in the following order.

## Phase 1 — body and pre-specialist evidence

Longstanding painful feet and walking difficulty become progressively joined by the GP to gait, tone, movement-initiation and functional deterioration.

**Suspicion state:** not yet a documentary-construction suspicion. The future question is what happens to an integrated problem once specialist categorisation begins.

## Phase 2 — inpatient neurological work

Neurology identifies severe very-young-onset Parkinsonism and recognises a lower-limb/footdrop-like appearance as potentially dystonic rather than true weakness. Structural foot abnormalities also remain real.

**Suspicion state:** the issue becomes integration rather than detection. The limb is neurologically meaningful; the future question is what work justifies treating the painful foot relationship as potentially separate.

## Phase 3 — Protest / decision-live moment

The patient's evidence is that, before discharge, he raised the simple objection that Neurology had not dealt with his feet.

The surviving specialist account does not record that exchange.

**Suspicion state:** if the event is accepted, incomplete integration becomes a decision-after-notice question. The Protest does not diagnose the problem; it shows that closure was challenged while a choice remained live.

## Phase 4 — document construction and finalisation

The final specialist product becomes much more elaborate than the patient's bedside information state. It contains high-resolution Parkinsonian/genetic reasoning and a possible separate/peripheral-neuropathy route for the painful feet.

The crucial unresolved issue is **authorship time**: when `leg pain`, `burning`, possible secondary peripheral neuropathy, alcohol linkage, `Not clear` and the sophisticated later synthesis first entered the documentary object and from what source.

**Suspicion state:** possible construction/documentary-dispositional closure. Still unproved until source/version evidence establishes chronology and provenance.

## Phase 5 — post-discharge treatment and independent specialist evidence

Substantial treatment response supports the reality and clinical importance of the Parkinsonian disorder. Independent specialist assessment supports the PARK2/Parkin direction.

At the same time, treatment withdrawal produces feet–gait–tension co-variation.

**Suspicion state:** broad diagnostic-error theories weaken; a narrower relationship/reopening question strengthens.

## Phase 6 — structural Podiatry stream

Podiatry repeatedly documents real mechanical pathology and provides real treatment.

**Suspicion state:** any exclusive neurological theory weakens. The proper case becomes mixed-mechanism and ownership-focused: who was responsible for joining mechanical and neurological information?

## Phase 7 — same-clinician contradiction thresholds

The treating neurologist later records or encounters potentially boundary-crossing evidence, including rapid foot change during withdrawal, a later footdrop-like phenomenon classified as Parkinsonian, OFF-related reasoning, Sinemet-related pain benefit and later controlled-release benefit.

**Suspicion state:** the centre moves from construction to continuing stewardship. The question becomes not whether later evidence existed, but what proposition actually lost confidence when it arrived.

## Phase 8 — treatment toxicity and counterweights

Visual toxicity, dyskinesia, behavioural effects, periods of substantial function and later pregabalin benefit create genuine innocent explanations and constrain simplistic treatment narratives.

**Suspicion state:** the case must narrow. It cannot depend on “all feet = Parkinsonism” or “more dopaminergic treatment should always have been given.”

## Phase 9 — 2023 severe pain and safety convergence

Severe bilateral pain, functional restriction and death-associated language coexist with genuine neurological reassessment, OFF/dystonia reasoning, neuropathy/Fabry consideration and treatment adjustment.

**Suspicion state:** “nothing was reconsidered” becomes untenable. The sharper question is whether local reassessment reconstructed the original integrated object or merely managed current branches.

## Phase 10 — 2024–2025 mixed-mechanism evidence

Pregabalin materially improves pain while toe curling persists. Sinemet is acknowledged to improve the feet from a pain perspective. More Sinemet is expected to help but is constrained by dyskinesia. Controlled-release Sinemet produces profound relief.

**Suspicion state:** the historical model is strongly pressured but not automatically falsified by one treatment response. The key question becomes effective falsifiability: what evidence would have required global reconstruction?

## Phase 11 — complaint and same-clinician retrospective explanation

The originating clinician later explains the longitudinal care and uses the accumulated history to support the reasonableness and continuity of the approach.

**Suspicion state:** a third stage appears — retrospective validation. The response is clinician evidence and may be correct, but it is not independent validation of the clinician's own earlier model merely because it is detailed and longitudinal.

## Phase 12 — PAR/SIMT and institutional review

Later institutional review concludes that there was no deficit in care.

**Suspicion state:** this is serious contrary evidence. Its weight depends on whether the review independently reconstructed the founding evidence, Protest, document-authorship chronology and later contradictions or primarily assessed the accumulated clinical account as already organised.

## Phase 13 — patient documentary discovery and reconstruction

The patient progressively obtains records and begins comparing lived history with institutional history. His own explanatory vocabulary and understanding evolve over time.

**Suspicion state:** patient-language change becomes a separate genealogy question. Later patient vocabulary cannot simply be projected backwards as original language, but neither can it be assumed to have been caused by the record without tracing the route.

## Phase 14 — data-governance and provenance testing

The patient begins using data-protection mechanisms to ask how the 2017 synthesis and later review were created and to seek durable association of his own supplementary account with the historical synthesis.

**Suspicion state:** the case becomes empirically testable at the information-system level. Provenance evidence can materially strengthen or weaken the construction limb; completion/association decisions can reveal how corrigible the historical information state is; PAR/SIMT provenance can show whether retrospective validation was independent or endogenous.

## Phase 15 — later genetic confirmation

Later genetics confirms Parkin/PRKN disease and dystonia/Parkinsonism.

**Suspicion state:** theories attacking the reality of Parkinsonism materially weaken. The surviving case is narrower: whether painful structural feet and Parkinsonian/dystonic lower-limb state were adequately related, represented, revisited and safeguarded at the relevant times.

# 5. The life-of-the-suspicion method

The repository must not read the record from the final theory backwards.

At every material threshold ask:

> **What did the suspicion look like immediately before this event?**

> **What new evidence entered the world?**

> **Does it strengthen, weaken, narrow, contradict or transform the suspicion?**

> **What innocent explanation does it preserve?**

> **What proposition should lose confidence because of it?**

> **What did the record actually do with that contradiction?**

> **Was the response a local update or a global reconstruction?**

> **Is the later statement independent evidence, interpretation, or a descendant of an earlier information state?**

A hypothesis that survives because contrary evidence was omitted is a repository failure.

A hypothesis that changes shape to accommodate contrary evidence may become stronger if the narrowing is evidence-led rather than rhetorical.

A hypothesis that the source record defeats should be marked defeated.

# 6. Current shape of the suspicion

The present suspicion has at least three substantive stages and one external testing layer.

## Construction

Did the final 2017 document convert an unfinished integrated feet–gait–tone problem into a portable specialist disposition before the underlying relationship had been adequately resolved, and did consequential wording arise before or after the Protest?

## Maintenance / continuing stewardship

Because the same neurologist may have remained centrally responsible, did later boundary-crossing evidence cause the founding formulation itself to change, or was it repeatedly accommodated locally?

## Retrospective validation

Did the original formulation partly generate the longitudinal record later relied upon as evidence that the original approach had been reasonable?

## Corrigibility / provenance testing

Can native authorship/version evidence, later review provenance and supplementary patient information materially alter how the historical record is understood and processed now?

This fourth layer is not a new allegation. It is an empirical way of testing the first three.

# 7. Patient language must have its own genealogy

The patient's later vocabulary must not be treated as timeless.

Track separately:

- original foot-pain and leg-tension language;
- appearance and provenance of `leg pain`;
- first identified `burning` wording in the July specialist synthesis;
- later contemporaneous patient use of burning language;
- later adoption of `neuralgic`, `neuropathic`, OFF/dystonia or other explanatory terms;
- changes after record access, complaint activity, Sinemet CR response and later genetics.

The evidential question is:

> **Which words came from lived experience, which from ordinary learning, which from clinicians, and which can actually be shown to descend from the authoritative record?**

Do not infer documentary influence merely because vocabulary converges.

# 8. What would materially weaken or defeat the major limbs?

## Construction limb

Materially weakened if native version history shows that the consequential terminology and neuropathy reasoning clearly pre-dated the Protest, arose from identifiable contemporaneous evidence, and followed adequate relationship-directed clinical work.

Materially strengthened if those elements first arose after the Protest, lack identifiable contemporaneous source, solve the precise closure problem raised by the Protest, and then shaped downstream information flow.

## Maintenance limb

Materially weakened if the longitudinal record shows a genuine global reconstruction of the original feet–gait–tone relationship in light of treatment-state, mechanical and later OFF/dopaminergic evidence, followed by a reasoned mixed-mechanism model.

Materially strengthened if repeated contradictions are expressly recognised yet consistently remain local exceptions while the higher-level founding architecture remains unchanged.

## Retrospective-validation limb

Materially weakened if later reviewers independently reconstruct primary 2017 evidence, Protest evidence, authorship chronology and later contradictions before reaching their conclusions.

Materially strengthened if later confidence rests materially on repeated descendant statements, same-clinician retrospective explanation or accumulated record coherence without independent reconstruction of the foundation.

---

# 9. Whole-case reader discipline

No reader should be allowed to say they know this case merely because they have read the final discharge letter, the complaint, the PAR/SIMT outcome, the later genetic result or the current repository formulation.

To know the case means being able to distinguish:

> **event → observation → interpretation → representation → inheritance → new observation → contradiction → revision/non-revision → complaint/review → provenance/correction attempt.**

The repository's job is to preserve those transformations.

Shortest rule:

> **Everything later must be traced either to new evidence entering the world or to an earlier information state being reproduced, interpreted, defended, corrected or challenged.**
