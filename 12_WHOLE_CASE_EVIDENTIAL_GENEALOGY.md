# 12 — Whole-Case Evidential Genealogy

## Status and purpose

**Active whole-case orientation file.**

This repository cannot responsibly discuss the case by starting from the finished July 2017 specialist account as though that document were the beginning of the evidence.

The central rule is:

> **The final specialist record is not the start of the case. It is itself an output of earlier evidence, encounters, decisions and document construction.**

The whole case must therefore be read genealogically: what entered the world first, what was observed, what was interpreted, what was written, what became portable, what was inherited, what was independently re-tested, what contemporaneous correspondence preserves outside the chart, what was later defended, and what is now being challenged through provenance and data-governance routes.

The dedicated correspondence layer is `16_CORRESPONDENCE_GENEALOGY_AND_CONTEMPORANEOUS_WITNESS.md`.

---

# 1. The true evidential start

The evidential start is not Parkinson's disease, PRKN, neuropathy, Podiatry or the final discharge synthesis.

The earliest patient-specific object is the lived and GP-observed problem that preceded specialist formulation:

> **painful abnormal feet + abnormal gait + increasing tone/movement difficulty + deteriorating function.**

The GP progressively brought those features together before Neurology. That pre-specialist material is therefore an independent comparator against which the later specialist representation can be tested.

The patient's experiential starting point is simpler:

> **My feet hurt and walking was becoming intolerable.**

Later terminology must not be projected backwards merely because it eventually became part of the record.

---

# 2. What may have been made to look like the start

A later reader receiving a polished specialist discharge product can experience it as the authoritative beginning of the story.

But the July 2017 final product follows:

1. the longstanding bodily problem;
2. GP observations and integrated referral;
3. inpatient encounter and bedside findings;
4. patient communications and experience of admission;
5. the alleged pre-discharge Protest;
6. physical discharge;
7. DaTscan/addendum activity;
8. transcription/finalisation/confirmation processes.

The finished document may therefore function as a **documentary start** for downstream readers without being the **evidential start** of the case.

# 3. Evidence genealogy classes

Every material later item should be classified by genealogy, not merely by date.

## A. Primary patient-state evidence

What the patient experienced, noticed, said, did, tolerated, improved with, worsened with, or remembers. Retrospective patient evidence remains evidence but is not automatically equivalent to contemporaneous documentation.

## B. Independent clinical observation

Examination, investigation, treatment-state observation or other patient-specific finding whose existence does not depend on accepting an earlier explanatory theory.

## C. Interpretive clinical proposition

A clinical explanation placed on observations: Parkinsonian, dystonic, structural, neuropathic, OFF-related, alcohol-related, mixed or otherwise. It proves what was thought, not automatically that the explanation was correct.

## D. Documentary output

A summary, referral, discharge synthesis, clinic letter, complaint response or other representation that selects and organises earlier material into a portable information state.

## E. Descendant narrative statement

A later statement whose wording, assumption or explanatory structure may descend from an earlier record rather than fresh independent assessment. Repetition can show continuity without proving independent confirmation.

## F. Independent re-evaluation

A later actor who actually re-examines the patient, underlying source material or founding reasoning and generates an assessment not merely inherited from the existing summary. Independence must be demonstrated.

## G. Governance/review output

Complaint findings, PAR/SIMT conclusions, QPS responses, regulatory decisions and similar institutional representations. Their weight on the underlying case depends on what they actually examined.

## H. Data-rights/provenance intervention

Article 15 access/provenance requests, Article 16 completion/rectification requests, Article 18 restriction activity, DPC complaints and related processing questions.

## I. Contemporaneous correspondence witness

Private or operational correspondence that preserves what the patient, clinician, practice, pharmacy, advocate or institution actually said or understood at a particular time outside the formal clinical note.

This class is especially important where later summaries risk becoming the only apparent account of the past.

Correspondence must still be classified by function:

- contemporaneous patient witness evidence;
- contemporaneous information-flow evidence;
- clinician/institutional evidence;
- later patient reconstruction;
- advocacy/submission history;
- regulatory/process evidence.

A contemporaneous email is not self-interpreting simply because it is time-stamped.

# 4. Whole-case sequence

## Phase 1 — body and pre-specialist evidence

Longstanding painful feet and walking difficulty become progressively joined by the GP to gait, tone, movement-initiation and functional deterioration. Private foot-care correspondence also shows practical foot treatment being sought before admission.

**Suspicion state:** no documentary-construction suspicion yet. The future question is what happens to an integrated problem once specialist categorisation begins.

## Phase 2 — inpatient neurological work

Neurology identifies severe very-young-onset Parkinsonism and recognises a lower-limb/footdrop-like appearance as potentially dystonic rather than true weakness. Structural foot abnormalities remain real.

**Suspicion state:** integration rather than detection becomes the issue.

## Phase 3 — Protest / decision-live moment

The patient gives a specific retrospective account that, before discharge, he raised the objection that Neurology had not dealt with his feet. The surviving specialist account does not record the exchange.

**Suspicion state:** if accepted, incomplete integration becomes a decision-after-notice question.

## Phase 4 — document construction and finalisation

The final specialist product contains high-resolution Parkinsonian/genetic reasoning and a possible separate/peripheral-neuropathy route for the painful feet.

The crucial unresolved issue is **authorship time**: when `leg pain`, `burning`, possible secondary peripheral neuropathy, alcohol linkage, `Not clear` and the sophisticated synthesis first entered the documentary object and from what source.

## Phase 5 — early post-discharge correspondence as contemporaneous witness

September–October 2017 private correspondence supplies a second contemporaneous vantage point.

It shows:

- strong patient acceptance of the Parkinsonian diagnosis and treatment;
- dramatic benefit;
- authentic patient use of `burning` by 4 October;
- rapid change in the patient's own causal understanding of the feet;
- feet–gait–tension co-variation during procyclidine withdrawal;
- active Podiatry/Physiotherapy/Neurology care;
- real medication toxicity;
- no presently identified mention of the Protest in a detailed private October narrative.

**Suspicion state:** several broad allegations weaken. The Protest remains patient evidence but now has meaningful contemporaneous negative evidence to be weighed. `Burning` remains a July provenance question, but not a viable clinician-only-language allegation.

## Phase 6 — post-discharge treatment and independent specialist evidence

Substantial treatment response supports the reality and importance of Parkinsonism. Independent specialist assessment supports the PARK2/Parkin direction.

**Suspicion state:** broad diagnostic-error theories weaken; relationship/reopening question strengthens.

## Phase 7 — structural Podiatry stream

Podiatry repeatedly documents real mechanical pathology and provides real treatment.

**Suspicion state:** exclusive neurological theories weaken; mixed mechanism and ownership become mandatory.

## Phase 8 — same-clinician contradiction thresholds

The treating neurologist later records or encounters rapid foot change during withdrawal, a footdrop-like phenomenon classified as Parkinsonian, OFF-related reasoning, Sinemet-related pain benefit and later controlled-release benefit.

**Suspicion state:** centre moves from construction to continuing stewardship: what proposition actually lost confidence when new evidence arrived?

## Phase 9 — treatment toxicity and counterweights

Visual toxicity, dyskinesia, behavioural effects, substantial periods of function and pregabalin benefit create genuine innocent explanations.

**Suspicion state:** case narrows. It cannot depend on “all feet = Parkinsonism” or “more dopaminergic treatment should always have been given.”

## Phase 10 — 2023 severe pain and safety convergence

A 22 March 2023 patient email directly says he is “losing the will to live” with the feet before later GP “death wish” language. Severe pain then coexists with genuine neurological reassessment, OFF/dystonia reasoning and contrary patient evidence about medication effect.

**Suspicion state:** safety becomes independently important; “nothing was reconsidered” becomes untenable.

## Phase 11 — 2024–March 2025 mixed-mechanism evidence

Pregabalin materially improves pain while movement phenomena persist. In an 18 March 2025 pre-complaint GP email the patient himself frames the problem as structural + neurological + neuropathic, records adjusted Sinemet timing and an existing half-Sinemet-CR-at-night plan, and asks for multidisciplinary reconstruction.

**Suspicion state:** later one-cause hindsight and “CR was never considered” theories weaken. Ownership/integration becomes stronger than discovery-of-one-mechanism.

## Phase 12 — complaint theory develops and itself becomes historical evidence

From March–April 2025 the patient begins complaint, access and external escalation activity. His theory becomes broader before later evidence narrows it again.

**Suspicion state:** the development of the allegation itself must be preserved. Advocacy documents prove what was alleged then; they are not independent proof of the underlying care.

## Phase 13 — real-world professional roles become clearer

Later correspondence supplies clinician evidence that Counihan advised Monaghan once in the 2017 weekly Complex Case meeting but did not have an ongoing management role; Counihan himself described himself in 2025 as not having been involved in the patient's care. Monaghan says he usually saw the patient himself over the years and gives a detailed longitudinal explanation.

**Suspicion state:** same-clinician custody strengthens; ongoing Counihan-responsibility theories weaken; the question becomes whether specialist advice that entered the case returned to the unresolved feet–gait relationship.

## Phase 14 — complaint and same-clinician retrospective explanation

The originating clinician later explains the care using the accumulated longitudinal record.

**Suspicion state:** retrospective validation becomes a distinct question. The response may be correct but is not independent validation merely because it is detailed.

## Phase 15 — Article 18, PAR/SIMT and continued institutional reliance

The patient raises a restriction/accuracy dispute on 4 August 2025. A PAR is later presented to SIMT on 27 August. SIMT concludes no deficit and no further review is required. In January 2026 QPS expressly maintains the validity of the completed process.

**Suspicion state:** this is serious contrary institutional evidence plus a concrete data-processing chronology. The proper question becomes what the PAR actually examined and what information state accompanied the data.

## Phase 16 — patient documentary discovery and reconstruction

Records access changes the patient's information state. His vocabulary and theory evolve further.

**Suspicion state:** later patient formulations cannot be projected backwards, but the record-access process itself becomes part of the case genealogy.

## Phase 17 — data-governance and provenance testing

Article 15/16/18 and DPC processes test source, authorship, review genealogy, restriction and supplementary completeness.

**Suspicion state:** construction and retrospective-validation hypotheses become externally falsifiable in new ways.

## Phase 18 — later genetic confirmation

Later genetics confirms Parkin/PRKN disease and dystonia/Parkinsonism.

**Suspicion state:** theories attacking the reality of Parkinsonism weaken. The surviving case becomes narrower: integration, representation, stewardship, mixed mechanism, safety and retrospective validation.

# 5. Life-of-the-suspicion method

At every material threshold ask:

> **What did the suspicion look like immediately before this event?**
>
> **What new evidence entered the world?**
>
> **Does it strengthen, weaken, narrow, contradict or transform the suspicion?**
>
> **What innocent explanation does it preserve?**
>
> **What proposition should lose confidence because of it?**
>
> **What did the record do with the contradiction?**
>
> **Was the response local updating or global reconstruction?**
>
> **Is the later statement independent evidence, interpretation, correspondence witness, or a descendant of an earlier information state?**

A hypothesis that survives because contrary evidence was omitted is a repository failure.

# 6. Current shape of the suspicion

## Construction

Did the final 2017 document convert an unfinished integrated feet–gait–tone problem into a portable specialist disposition before the relationship had been adequately resolved, and did consequential wording arise before or after the Protest?

## Maintenance / continuing stewardship

Did later boundary-crossing evidence cause the founding formulation itself to change, or was it repeatedly accommodated locally?

## Retrospective validation

Did the original formulation partly generate the longitudinal record later relied upon as evidence that the original approach was reasonable?

## Corrigibility / provenance testing

Can native authorship/version evidence, review provenance and supplementary patient information materially alter how the historical record is understood and processed now?

# 7. Patient language must have its own genealogy

The correspondence review materially improves this audit.

Track:

- original foot-pain and leg-tension language;
- anomalous July `leg pain`;
- July `burning` in the specialist etiological paragraph;
- **independent private patient use of `burning` by 4 October 2017**;
- patient's October statement that he had only very recently begun to reinterpret feet through Parkinsonism;
- later `neuralgic`, `neuropathic`, OFF/dystonia and other terms;
- later changes after record access, complaint activity, Sinemet CR response and genetics.

The question is:

> **Which words came from lived experience, which from ordinary learning, which from clinicians, and which can actually be shown to descend from the authoritative record?**

# 8. What would materially weaken or defeat the major limbs?

## Construction limb

Weakened if native versions show consequential terminology and neuropathy reasoning clearly pre-dated the Protest, arose from identifiable contemporaneous evidence, and followed adequate relationship-directed clinical work.

## Maintenance limb

Weakened if the longitudinal record shows genuine global reconstruction of the original relationship in light of treatment-state, mechanical and OFF/dopaminergic evidence.

## Retrospective-validation limb

Weakened if later reviewers independently reconstruct primary 2017 evidence, Protest evidence, authorship chronology and contradictions before reaching their conclusions.

# 9. Whole-case reader discipline

No reader knows this case merely by reading the discharge letter, complaint, SIMT outcome, genetics or current theory.

To know the case means being able to distinguish:

> **event → observation → correspondence witness → interpretation → representation → inheritance → new observation → contradiction → revision/non-revision → complaint/review → provenance/correction attempt.**

Shortest rule:

> **Everything later must be traced either to new evidence entering the world or to an earlier information state being reproduced, interpreted, defended, corrected or challenged.**
