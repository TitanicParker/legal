# 09 — Case Study Development Log

## Purpose

This log records material changes in the case-study formulation and repository architecture. It is not a diary of conversations and it is not evidence that a proposition is true merely because the proposition appears here.

Create an entry when a change materially affects source accuracy/provenance, chronology, the strongest suspicion or a material competing explanation, an expert-dependent question, an ethical/legal/regulatory/patient-safety route, an external submission/response, or the status of a hypothesis.

The governing standard is:

> **Preserve provenance, expose uncertainty, record correction, permit falsification, distinguish repetition from independent confirmation, and never mistake documentary coherence for truth.**

## 11 September 2026 — `burning` provenance hypothesis materially narrowed

**Trigger/source:** private patient email dated 4 October 2017.

**What changed:** the email uses the patient's own words **“scorching hot and filled with burning pain.”** The repository can no longer responsibly imply that `burning` could only have been clinician-generated.

**What remains unresolved:** how and when `burning` entered the earlier July specialist synthesis. October evidence cannot be projected backward into July.

**Effect on formulation:** construction hypothesis narrowed. `leg pain` now remains the sharper textual/provenance anomaly; `burning` remains a July authorship/source question, not a clinician-only vocabulary theory.

## 11 September 2026 — October 2017 private narrative added as both supportive and contrary evidence

**Trigger/source:** detailed private patient narrative of 4 October 2017.

**What strengthened:** contemporaneous evidence of severe historical foot pain, authentic burning vocabulary, strong treatment benefit, changing causal understanding and early feet–gait–tension medication-state co-variation.

**What weakened:** broad theories of an obviously failed Parkinsonian diagnosis; any timeless reconstruction of the patient's present theory; overconfidence that the patient always understood the feet in the same way.

**New contrary evidence:** the detailed narrative does not presently record the alleged Protest. That omission is preserved as **limited negative evidence**, not proof of non-occurrence.

**Effect:** Protest evidential assessment becomes more nuanced rather than weaker by fiat.

## 11 September 2026 — Medical Council PPC material-considered provenance bounded

**Trigger/source:** Medical Council secure-delivery email of 16 February 2026 and the follow-up correspondence of 16–17 February 2026 concerning the PPC minute.

**What changed:** the repository can now verify that an extract of a Preliminary Proceedings Committee minute dated **29 January 2026** existed and that the minute recorded consideration of Dr Monaghan's responses dated **29 July 2025 and 29 August 2025**. The Medical Council's Authorised Officer explained on 17 February that the 29 August email was Monaghan stating that he wished to use his 29 July submission as his response to the complaint. The officer also stated that the PPC had not directed that the 29 August email itself be supplied to the patient.

**What this establishes:** the 29 July clinician response was expressly reaffirmed by Monaghan for use as his complaint response and was part of the material before the PPC. The 29 August item did not, on the Council's account, add a new substantive clinical explanation.

**What remains unresolved:** the secure ShareFile delivery identified a 16 February 2026 Council letter and the 29 January PPC minute, but their full substantive text is not presently readable through the Gmail attachment path available in this audit. The repository therefore does not infer or state the PPC's final merits disposition.

**Effect on formulation:** strengthens the provenance map of later regulatory reliance without changing the underlying clinical merits. It also reinforces the rule that **clinician response → regulator consideration** is not the same evidential step as **independent regulator verification**.
