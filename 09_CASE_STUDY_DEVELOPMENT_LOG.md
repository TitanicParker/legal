# 09 — Case Study Development Log

## Purpose

This log records material changes in the case-study formulation and repository architecture. It is not a diary of conversations and it is not evidence that a proposition is true merely because the proposition appears here.

Create an entry when a change materially affects one or more of the following:

- source accuracy or provenance;
- the controlling factual chronology;
- the strongest suspicion or a material competing explanation;
- an expert-dependent question;
- an ethical, legal, regulatory or patient-safety route;
- an external submission or response;
- the status of a hypothesis as strengthened, weakened, falsified, abandoned or still unresolved.

For each material change, preserve what changed, why it changed, what evidence caused the change, and what remained unresolved. Where a prior formulation was wrong, record the correction rather than silently smoothing the history.

The governing standard is:

> **Preserve provenance, expose uncertainty, record correction, permit falsification, distinguish repetition from independent confirmation, and never mistake documentary coherence for truth.**

## 11 September 2026 — repository becomes an explicit developing case study

**Trigger:** the project was reframed from a medico-legal working file into a repository that preserves both the clinical/documentary case and the process by which the case is developed, challenged, corrected and externally tested.

**Repository change:** `01_CASE_STUDY_CHARTER.md` was added and made the governance statement for that dual purpose.

**Effect on formulation:** no clinical allegation was proved by the organisational change. The repository gained an explicit requirement to retain contrary evidence, distinguish evidence classes, preserve historical development and allow important hypotheses to fail under scrutiny.

**Status:** governance change; no evidential status change.

## 11 September 2026 — strongest documentary/dispositional suspicion made explicit

**Trigger:** the patient required the strongest present suspicion to be tested rather than diluted into a generic diagnostic-error theory.

**Repository change:** the top of `README.md` was expanded to state the suspicion that specialist sophistication may be relevant not only to documentary closure but also to **dispositional closure**: the possibility that an unresolved painful-foot manifestation was given authoritative alternative explanatory/service routes that reduced the likelihood of reconstruction at the specialist level capable of resolving the integrated neurological question.

**Control:** this remains a suspicion, not a finding of intention, dishonesty, manipulation, breach or causation. Its strongest proof/falsification route is documentary provenance and authorship timing, especially the origin and timing of `leg pain`, `burning`, the possible-neuropathy branch, the Protest, and final document construction.

**Effect on formulation:** strengthens the question to be examined; does not change the evidential burden.
