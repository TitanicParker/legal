# 20 — Proposition Proof Ledger

## Purpose

This file is the repository's proof spine.

Every material proposition should be capable of being traced to:

- its source;
- its evidential class;
- the inference it permits;
- what it does not need to prove;
- what could disprove it;
- and where it is used.

The goal is to stop repetition from masquerading as proof and to stop caveats from dissolving propositions that are already established at the proper level.

# Use rule

Substantive files should cite proposition IDs where possible rather than restating the entire evidential debate.

Example:

> **The founding asymmetry is supported by P01–P05. Early reopening pressure is supported by P06–P07. Longitudinal neurological reintegration is supported by P08–P11. Same-clinician stewardship is supported by P12–P13.**

This allows the merits case to accumulate without carrying every caveat inline.

---

# Expansion rule

When a new material proposition enters the repository:

1. assign a stable ID;
2. identify its evidence class;
3. identify exact source anchor(s);
4. state the narrowest permitted inference;
5. state what it does not need to prove;
6. state the strongest evidence that could defeat it;
7. add cross-references from the files that use it.
