# 22 — Evidence Acquisition Priority Queue

## Purpose

This is the ranked queue of missing evidence most capable of changing the governing suspicion.

Priority is based on expected evidential value, not convenience or drama.

---

# Priority 1 — July 2017 native version / audit provenance

**Why first:** this is the decisive evidence for the graver post-Protest construction limb.

**Need:** native versions, dictation/transcription metadata, audit history, revision timestamps, autosaves, sign-off/addendum chronology, transmitted versions.

**Can strengthen:** post-Protest construction / knowing closure suspicion.

**Can weaken/defeat:** same limb, if consequential wording clearly pre-dated Protest and arose from identifiable contemporaneous clinical work.

---

# Priority 2 — PAR/SIMT source genealogy

**Why second:** institutional `no deficit` conclusion is currently important adverse evidence whose weight cannot be calibrated without knowing what was actually reviewed.

**Need:** PAR, terms, sources, drafts, clinical inputs, independent specialist input, SIMT papers/minutes/questions/rationale.

**Can strengthen:** retrospective-validation/endogenous-coherence suspicion if review relied materially on inherited record and respondent synthesis.

**Can weaken:** central suspicion if independent review reconstructed and answered the same integration/stewardship problem.

---

# Priority 3 — Event-level Monaghan knowledge/authorship map

**Why third:** same-clinician continuity is central, but broad continuity is not enough for every threshold.

**Need:** authorship/attendance/routing metadata for Apr 2018, 2020, 2023, 2025 and other key observations.

**Can strengthen:** stewardship/non-revision case.

**Can weaken:** same-clinician attribution at particular thresholds if evidence shows other clinicians exclusively owned the relevant observations.

---

# Priority 4 — Independent movement-disorders expert opinion

**Why fourth:** this converts documentary predicates into or away from breach propositions.

**Questions:**

- what relationship-directed examination was required in 2017?
- was the separate/neuropathy branch clinically earned?
- when did later crossing evidence require re-phenotyping/global reconstruction?
- how should mixed structural and neurological mechanisms have been integrated?
- what treatment/safety consequences follow from any identified departure?

**Can strengthen or defeat:** the central integration/stewardship limb directly.

---

# Priority 5 — Teaching/student provenance of the Protest

**Why fifth:** independent corroboration would materially strengthen decision-after-notice and potentially construction chronology.

**Need:** rosters, teaching logs, cohort records, supervisor schedules, retained student material, lawful witness identification.

**Can strengthen:** Protest occurrence/date and institutional context.

**Can weaken:** if reliable contrary evidence emerges.

---

# Priority 6 — Objective neuropathy evidence

**Why sixth:** this is one of the most important possible substantive rebuttals to the founding route.

**Need:** NCS/EMG, sensory exams, neuropathy investigations, explicit diagnostic basis, later specialist evidence.

**Can weaken:** theory that neuropathy branch was weakly founded.

**Does not automatically defeat:** integration/stewardship if neurological treatment-state effects coexisted.

---

# Priority 7 — WordPress/Facebook publication provenance

**Why seventh:** would externally time-fix 2017 symptom phenomenology, especially the left-foot twisting observation.

**Need:** WordPress metadata/revisions, Facebook activity/share traces, archive/cache, comments/reactions.

**Can strengthen:** uncontaminated contemporaneous evidence of dynamic foot phenomenology.

**Does not establish:** clinician notice.

---

# Priority 8 — Full Medical Council PPC primary text

**Why eighth:** needed to distinguish procedure, evidence considered and actual regulatory disposition without inference from correspondence around it.

**Need:** 16 Feb 2026 letter + 29 Jan 2026 PPC minute in readable primary form.

**Can change:** regulatory-response analysis, not directly clinical merits unless the decision reveals what evidence was independently examined.

---

# Priority 9 — Full service-ownership map

**Why ninth:** tests the proposition that nobody durably owned the arrows between structural pathology, neurological state and pain.

**Need:** referral/follow-up arrangements, MDT communications, discharge responsibilities, re-referral criteria, Podiatry/Neurology feedback loops.

**Can strengthen or defeat:** diffusion-of-relational-ownership limb.

---

# Priority 10 — Collateral memory accuracy ledger

**Why tenth:** relevant mainly to Protest credibility, not the central integration case.

**Need:** later recollections demonstrably made before rediscovery of independently fixed contemporary records.

**Can strengthen:** weight of retrospective patient recollection.

**Cannot prove:** Protest merely by general credibility.

---

# Queue rule

When evidence arrives:

1. update `19_LIVE_SUSPICION_STATUS_LEDGER.md`;
2. update the relevant proposition(s) in `20_PROPOSITION_PROOF_LEDGER.md`;
3. update `09_CASE_STUDY_DEVELOPMENT_LOG.md` only if the formulation materially changes;
4. then revise narrative files.

Do not rewrite the theory before updating the evidential status.
