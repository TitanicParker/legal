# 11 — Same-Clinician Knowledge, Contradiction and Revision Ledger

## Status and purpose

**Active suspicion-led longitudinal audit.**

The purpose of this file is not to rehearse general balance. It is to answer a narrower question:

> **What did the originating neurologist know before each threshold, what new evidence arrived, what existing proposition did that evidence challenge, what changed, and was the change local or global?**

Monaghan's 29 July 2025 Medical Council response states that he **usually saw the patient himself** over the relevant years. That materially strengthens same-clinician continuity, while event-level authorship and knowledge still require source mapping.

This file therefore treats the case as a stewardship problem rather than a generic downstream-inheritance problem.

---

# 1. Unit of analysis

For every significant threshold identify:

1. **Prior knowledge state** — what Monaghan already knew or had authored/approved.
2. **New evidence** — what new observation, report, treatment response or contradiction arrived.
3. **Proposition challenged** — what existing explanatory proposition should have lost confidence, if any.
4. **Actual response** — examination, treatment, documentation, referral, explanation or no identified change.
5. **Revision level** — local update or global reconstruction.
6. **Residual issue** — what remained unresolved afterwards.

The central discipline is:

> **Do not ask only what happened next. Ask what proposition changed because of what happened next.**

# 4. Current longitudinal inference

The record demonstrates genuine reassessment.

It therefore does **not** support a theory that Neurology never revisited the feet.

What it presently supports is more exact:

> **Later evidence repeatedly changed the interpretation or management of individual foot/lower-limb phenomena, but the repository has not identified a corresponding explicit reconstruction of the founding whole relationship among painful feet, dystonic/Parkinsonian lower-limb behaviour, structural pathology, loading and neurological treatment state.**

That is the core maintenance proposition.

---

# 5. The proposition-loss test

For each threshold, ask:

> **What proposition actually lost confidence because of this evidence?**

Possible answers include:

- the foot phenomenon is mechanically fixed;
- the foot phenomenon is peripheral weakness;
- foot symptoms are independent of neurological treatment state;
- pain is unrelated to OFF state;
- dopaminergic state does not affect the feet;
- the founding separation remains a sufficient higher-level explanation.

If the record shows that lower-level propositions repeatedly changed while the higher-level architecture remained untouched, that is relevant to the suspicion of **local updating without global reconstruction**.

It is not, by itself, proof of intent.

---

# 6. Global reconstruction test

A genuine global reconstruction would be expected to do more than manage an episode.

It would expressly revisit the original integrated problem and state, in substance:

- which foot phenomena were structural;
- which were dystonic/Parkinsonian;
- which were neuropathic or centrally amplified;
- how loading and abnormal posture interacted;
- how treatment state affected pain/movement;
- who owned the combined problem;
- what earlier propositions were revised;
- what follow-up/reopening criteria followed.

The presently available record does not identify such a synthesis.

If one exists, it is high-priority contrary evidence and should be added immediately to `19_LIVE_SUSPICION_STATUS_LEDGER.md`.

# 9. 2025 clinician explanation as an epistemic event

Monaghan's 2025 Medical Council response is important evidence of his account of the longitudinal care.

It also has a distinct structure:

> **founding formulation → later observations → later documentation → treatment decisions → retrospective same-clinician explanation using the accumulated record.**

The response may be correct. But where the allegation concerns the organising effect of the founding formulation, the same clinician's later synthesis of the record is not automatically independent validation of that formulation.

This is the retrospective-validation issue, not a motive allegation.

---

# 10. What evidence could defeat the maintenance limb

The maintenance/stewardship limb would materially weaken if:

1. a genuine whole-case reconstruction is found;
2. key boundary-crossing observations did not in fact reach Monaghan;
3. durable multidisciplinary ownership of the integrated relationship is demonstrated elsewhere;
4. expert evidence concludes that the local updates were clinically sufficient and no global reconstruction was required.

These are real defeat conditions.

General evidence that structural pathology, treatment toxicity or mixed mechanisms existed does not by itself answer the revision question.

---

# 11. Current control proposition

> **The question is no longer only who inherited the frame. It is what its author allowed to change it. The presently available record shows repeated same-system local revisions in response to foot/lower-limb evidence crossing the founding partition; it does not presently show an explicit whole-case reconstruction of the relationship that had originally been separated. The maintenance limb therefore turns on event-level knowledge, proposition loss and revision level, not on a broad claim that nothing was reconsidered.**
