# 90 — Method, Evidence and Falsification

## Purpose

This file contains the repository's controlling evidential discipline.

Substantive merits files should cross-reference this method rather than reproduce the same cautions repeatedly.

The repository's task is to make the strongest case the evidence supports, while preserving the conditions by which that case can be tested, narrowed or defeated.

---

# 1. Governing rule

> **Establish the proposition at the level the evidence permits. Do not weaken it merely to display caution. Do not strengthen it beyond the evidence merely to display advocacy.**

A qualification belongs beside an affirmative proposition only if omitting it would make that proposition materially false or misleading.

Other defences and limitations belong in `80_DEFENCE_AND_LIMITATIONS.md`.

---

# 2. Evidence classes

Use these classes consistently:

- **DOCUMENTARY FACT** — directly established by available records.
- **PATIENT EVIDENCE** — patient recollection, testimony or contemporaneous account.
- **CLINICIAN EVIDENCE** — clinician-authored observation, recollection or explanation.
- **REASONABLE INFERENCE** — inference drawn from established material.
- **DISPUTED FACT** — proposition whose factual status is contested or uncorroborated.
- **EXPERT-DEPENDENT MEDICAL PROPOSITION** — requires appropriate independent clinical expertise.
- **DOCUMENTARY/FORENSIC QUESTION** — requires native files, metadata, audit trail, version history or authorship evidence.
- **ETHICAL QUESTION/OPINION** — requires ethical analysis and does not itself establish legal liability.
- **LEGAL QUESTION/OPINION** — requires applicable law and legal expertise.
- **UNSUPPORTED ALLEGATION** — presently lacks sufficient foundation.

Serious allegations about intention, concealment, manipulation, dishonesty or interference must never be converted into documentary fact by repetition.

---

# 3. Source hierarchy

1. Original/native source document, image, system entry, metadata or audit trail where available.
2. Verified source-to-transcription corrections.
3. `REPAIRED_CANONICAL_V2.md` as current working transcription where no discrepancy has been identified.
4. Contemporaneous patient and clinician communications.
5. Focused evidence maps and audits.
6. Integrated formulations.
7. Expert analyses and external opinions.
8. Submission documents and advocacy narratives.

No downstream formulation overrules a verified primary source.

---

# 4. Two chronologies

Always distinguish:

## Clinical-event chronology

What happened to the patient, in what order, and what information was available at each moment.

## Document-authorship chronology

When each consequential phrase, inference, addendum, synthesis element or finalised version was actually created, revised and transmitted.

The finished order of paragraphs is not evidence of authorship order.

---

# 5. Cumulative proof

Do not require each event independently to prove the entire case.

Ask what inference becomes reasonable from the sequence as a whole.

In this case, the relevant cumulative chain includes:

> **integrated referral → dystonic-looking lower-limb recognition → diagnostic use of that feature → separate/neuropathy disposition for the painful-foot relationship → early treatment-state crossing → same-clinician focal foot change → later Parkinsonian foot classification → OFF-related pain/shuffling → explicit dopaminergic pain benefit → local updating without presently demonstrated whole-case reconstruction.**

Evidence should be permitted to accumulate before rebuttal is applied.

---

# 6. Local updating versus global reconstruction

This distinction should be used affirmatively where the record permits it.

- **Local updating** — a new episode, symptom or treatment-state observation is recognised and managed within the existing architecture.
- **Global reconstruction** — the higher-level relationship among the original presenting domains is expressly reformulated in light of accumulated contradictory or cross-boundary evidence.

Where the repository has searched the record and found repeated local updates but no explicit global reconstruction, the responsible statement is:

> **The presently available record demonstrates repeated local updating; it does not presently demonstrate a corresponding global reconstruction of the founding relationship.**

Do not unnecessarily convert that proposition back into an open question.

---

# 7. Protest evidence

The alleged pre-discharge Protest is direct retrospective patient evidence of a disputed historical event.

Its status should be assessed through `18_PROTEST_EMERGENCE_AND_INSTITUTIONAL_CONDITIONS.md`.

Do not repeat the same retrospective-evidence caveat throughout unrelated files.

Silence is not inconsistency unless there is a reason to expect the fact to appear in the earlier document or occasion.

---

# 8. Treatment response

Treatment response can establish treatment-state dependence without establishing one exclusive mechanism.

Use response evidence for the proposition it actually supports.

Do not demand mechanism proof from every treatment-state observation before allowing it to bear on integration or reopening.

---

# 9. Structural disease and mixed mechanisms

Genuine structural pathology should not be treated as a universal rebuttal to neurological integration.

Mixed pathology may be clinically more complex and may increase the need for clear ownership of the relationship between domains.

The relevant question is not whether mechanical pathology existed. It did.

The relevant question is whether the neurological and mechanical truths were adequately integrated and later reconstructed.

---

# 10. Same-clinician continuity

Continuity should be proved at the level the source permits.

A broad clinician statement that he usually saw the patient supports substantial longitudinal continuity.

It does not establish authorship or knowledge of every event.

Use event-level source mapping where a specific knowledge proposition matters.

Do not repeatedly add unrelated warnings about bad faith or intent where the merits issue is knowledge, opportunity to revise or stewardship.

---

# 11. Institutional review

An institutional conclusion is evidence that the institution reached that conclusion.

Its weight on the underlying case depends on what evidence the process actually examined and how independently it reconstructed the relevant issue.

Do not treat accumulated record coherence as independent validation without examining provenance.

---

# 12. Failure conditions

The central case must remain falsifiable.

Material defeat conditions include:

- expert evidence that the 2017 relational assessment was clinically adequate;
- proof of a later genuine global reconstruction that the repository has missed;
- proof of durable relational ownership across services;
- objective later neuropathy evidence materially vindicating the founding pathway;
- independent review demonstrating that the same integration/stewardship allegation was actually reconstructed and answered;
- native authorship/version evidence defeating the post-Protest construction suspicion.

These failure conditions should be stated once here and in the dedicated defence file, not repeated in every substantive merits document.

---

# 13. Advocacy hierarchy

The proper order is:

1. what happened;
2. what the central alleged wrong is;
3. the strongest evidence establishing it;
4. the longitudinal consequences;
5. later institutional reliance;
6. genuine defence evidence;
7. limitations and defeat conditions;
8. expert/legal questions.

Fairness does not require claimant and defence voices to appear simultaneously in every paragraph.

---

# 14. Repository rule

> **One subject, one controlling caveat location.**

Examples:

- Protest credibility: `18_PROTEST_EMERGENCE_AND_INSTITUTIONAL_CONDITIONS.md`
- terminology provenance: `06_TERMINOLOGY_PROVENANCE_AUDIT.md`
- correspondence witness: `16_CORRESPONDENCE_GENEALOGY_AND_CONTEMPORANEOUS_WITNESS.md`
- data governance: `13_LIVE_DATA_GOVERNANCE_AND_PROVENANCE_TRACK.md`
- general defence/limitations: `80_DEFENCE_AND_LIMITATIONS.md`
- general methodology/falsification: this file.

Substantive files should cross-reference rather than reproduce long caveat lists.

---

# 15. End test

A mature formulation should be able to answer three questions without self-interruption:

> **What happened?**

> **Why does the evidence support that account?**

> **What genuine evidence could still defeat it?**

That is sufficient evidential discipline.
